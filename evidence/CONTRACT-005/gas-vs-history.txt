gas of ReputationRegistry.getSummary against one client's history length
=======================================================================

Measured 2026-09-23 against MockReputationRegistry, whose getSummary is
transcribed from erc-8004-contracts@b9e466c and loops every entry of every
listed client. Raw run: evidence/CONTRACT-005/forge-test.log
(test_GasAgainstHistoryLength).

  entries       gas   marginal

        5    62,440      8,588/entry
       20   191,267      8,588/entry
       50   448,932      8,589/entry
      100   878,402      8,589/entry

Fixed cost ~19,499, marginal ~8,589 per feedback entry, linear.

Consequence for the default ceiling of 250,000 gas: a trusted client with more
than about 26 entries for one agent is dropped from the gate, and ten such
clients would cost ~2.5M gas in fund() alone. That is a property of ERC-8004's
reputation read, not of this escrow -- and it is exactly why the read is capped
rather than trusted. Raising the ceiling raises the cost of every fund();
lowering it below MIN_REPUTATION_READ_GAS (45,000, about 2 entries) is refused,
because a ceiling low enough to starve every read would switch the gate off
while leaving it looking applied.

For DOC-004 (limitations) and EVAL-002 (gas).
